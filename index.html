    FAQ

    The details,
    unwrapped.

    What is the minimum order?

    20 boxes without branding, 50 with your logo on the sleeve. Hotels start from a free test kit of 50 welcome chocolates.

    How long does it take?

    10 working days after you approve the mock-up. A fully custom box takes 3–4 weeks. Express production takes 7 working days for +15%. For December, order by November 25.

    Can you deliver to each employee’s home?

    Yes. Send us a list of addresses and we ship each box separately across the EU and the USA, +€6 per address.

    How long do the sweets stay fresh?

    Six months. Every order leaves us with at least four months remaining.

    Are they suitable for diabetics or allergies?

    All sweets are sugar-free. Vegan and nut-free lines are made separately, and every box carries a full allergen card.

    Can we taste first?

    Order a sample box for €15. It arrives in 3–5 days and we deduct the price from your first order.

    How do we pay?

    By invoice with VAT. New clients pay 50% upfront, regular clients within 14 days, hotel contracts within 30 days.
